# Liquid Utilities

---

**Short Description:**

Liquid Utilities (LU for short) is a KSP mod that allows planet modders to do more with the liquids on their planets.

---

**Features:**

- Liquid density (Ex: Salt water would have a 1.025 density which makes you float higher)
- Liquid Drag (Self explainable, can be used for swamps or such)
- Map support (Allows you to have maps of lakes, oceans, rivers, etc on planets, then you can set their properties in the config)
- Coordinate support (Same purpose as Map, but an alternative)
- Toolbar button (Used to see if your configs actually applied to where you are properly)

**Future Features:**

- Bodies of water can have differing altitudes

---

Example Mod:

Kerbol Liquid Changes

KLC basically just changes the water on Laythe / Kerbin to be Salt / Fresh water depending on the area. Not all Kerbin or Laythe lakes are supported, this mod is more just used to show what Liquid Utilities can do. It most likely will be updated as new features come out for LU.
